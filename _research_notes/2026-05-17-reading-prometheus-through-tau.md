## How to Read This Note

Read this as a structural articulation, not a critique. PROMETHEUS makes a real engineering move: it turns retrieved corpora into sheaf-like causal atlases of local PSRs over context covers, with a four-fold gluing diagnostic. The categorical machinery is invoked heuristically — the §7 "operational sheaf condition" is an averaging rule with a numerical compatibility check, not a Mac Lane–Moerdijk sheaf condition. The four-fold diagnostic is left as an ad-hoc taxonomy. The predicate "Belnap" appears nowhere in the references.

The τ-canon reading takes the engineering observation seriously and articulates the structural ingredients that turn its heuristic invocation into a proven construction.

## Three Structural Contributions

1. **The 4-fold gluing diagnostic IS the earned subobject classifier.** Theorem I.T25 of Book I proves that `Ω_τ = Truth4 = {T, F, B, N}` is the unique subobject classifier of `PSh(Cat_τ)`, with the orthogonality `e_+ · e_- = 0` (where `e_± = (1 ± j)/2` are the bipolar idempotents and `j² = +1` is the split-complex unit of the boundary algebra) *earned* from spectral structure rather than imposed by fiat. PROMETHEUS's four diagnostics map cleanly onto Belnap-Dunn four-valued logic, with one precise mismatch (Contradiction maps to `B`, not `F`) that the structural reading exposes.

2. **Exact gluing is achievable, not merely approximate.** Theorem III.T50 of Book III proves the Global Cartesian Gluing as a colimit ("canonically isomorphic", not ε-approximate). The structural ingredient PROMETHEUS lacks is the boundary functor `Φ_∂` (Langlands Coherent Force) that upgrades approximate to exact.

3. **The Para-Mind reading licenses the external-topos construction.** Definition VII.D55 of Book VII characterises Large Language Models as *para-minds*: pattern processors over the subsymbolic presheaf `M_sub` that lack three constitutive features (no internal topos, no self-model, no commitment register). PROMETHEUS's externalization of the topos layer is therefore structurally licensed by the para-mind status of its LLM substrate.

## One Constructive Structural Prediction

PROMETHEUS already carries the `polarity` field in its causal-claim schema (cSQL), but does not currently use it to drive a fifth gluing diagnostic. The τ-canon reading predicts that activating the polarity field as a refutation signal would yield a clean Belnap-`F` state and complete the 4-valued classifier readout.
