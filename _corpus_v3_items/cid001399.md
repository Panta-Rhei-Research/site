    \ ensures $\\chi_G$ is idempotent-supported;\n          \\textup{II.T37} produces the unique extension.\n\n    \\item[\\\

    chi}^+\n    \\;+\\;\n    e_- \\cdot G_{f_\\chi}^-,\n\\]\nwhere $G_{f_\\chi}^\\pm$\ndepends only on $\\chi_\\pm$.\n```\n\
