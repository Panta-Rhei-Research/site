    # Bridge Necessary Insufficient


    ## Summary


    Bridge necessary but insufficient. All gaps map to exponential_quantification with damage 3 (break). Bridge cannot create
    analytic content needed for infinite case.


    ## Statement


    ```latex

    \label{thm:bridge-necessary}

    The bridge axiom is \emph{necessary} for connecting $\tau$-internal

    results to external conjectures: all three gaps map to

    \texttt{exponential\_quantification} with bridge damage~3 (break).

    But the bridge is \emph{insufficient}: even with the bridge functor,

    the analytic content (circle method, sieve asymptotics, height theory)

    needed for the infinite case is absent.
    ```
