    \         the entry $c^\\pm_k$ is a finite family\n          of spectral coefficients:\n          \\[\n              c^\\\
    pm_k\n              \\;=\\;\n              \\bigl\\{\\,\n              \\varphi_{p,v}^{(\\pm)}\n              \\;\\big|\\\
    ;\n              p \\mid P_k,\\;\n              v \\in \\mathbb{Z}/p\\mathbb{Z}\n              \\,\\bigr\\},\n       \
    \   \\]\n          with each $\\varphi_{p,v}^{(+)} \\in A_\\tau^{(B)}$\n          and each $\\varphi_{p,v}^{(-)} \\in\
    \ A_\\tau^{(C)}$.\n          Here $(+)$ refers to the B-channel\n          (associated with $e_+$)\n          and $(-)$\
    \ to the C-channel\n          (associated with $e_-$).\n\n    \\item[\\textup{(C2)}]\n          \\textbf{Tower coherence.}\n\
