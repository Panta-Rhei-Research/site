    # Hodge Requires E₁


    ## Summary


    At E₀, all characters are NF-addressable (Book II Central Theorem). The Hodge question becomes non-trivial only at E₁,
    where sector admissibility predicates distinguish addressable-within-sector from addressable-globally.


    ## Statement


    ```latex

    \label{prop:hodge-requires-e1}

    At enrichment level~$E_0$, every boundary character $\chi \in \operatorname{Char}(\mathbb{L})$ is globally NF-addressable.
    The Hodge question---which characters are sector-addressable---becomes non-trivial only at~$E_1$, where the $4{+}1$ sector
    decomposition introduces sector-specific admissibility predicates that are not present at~$E_0$.

    ```
