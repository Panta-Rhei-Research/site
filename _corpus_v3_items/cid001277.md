    Let $f = \\{f_k\\}_{k \\geq 1}$\nbe an $\\omega$-germ transformer\non~$\\tau^3$,\nwith bipolar decomposition\n$f = e_+\
    \ f_+ + e_- f_-$.\nThe \\textbf{Laurent expansion} of~$f$\nat a $\\tau$-admissible point~$x$\nis the formal double series:\n\
    \\[\n    \\boxed{%\n    f\n    \\;=\\;\n    \\sum_{n \\in \\mathbb{Z}}\n    a_n^{(+)}\\, e_+\\, \\phi_n^{(+)}\n    \\\
    ;\\;+\\;\\;\n    \\sum_{n \\in \\mathbb{Z}}\n    a_n^{(-)}\\, e_-\\, \\phi_n^{(-)},}\n\\]\nwhere the \\textbf{spectral\
