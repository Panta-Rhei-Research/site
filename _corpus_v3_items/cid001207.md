    Let $T \\in \\mathrm{HolFun}$.\nTower coherence\n(Definition~\\ref{def:tower-coherence}, I.D46)\nstates that for all $k\

    bigr).\n\\]\nThis is the naturality square for $T : \\mathcal{F} \\Rightarrow \\mathcal{F}_{\\jj}$:\n\\[\n    \\begin{array}{ccc}\n\
