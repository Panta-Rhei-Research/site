    $\\{M_{1}, \\ldots, M_{5}\\}$ be the five forbidden moves\n(Definition~\\ref{def:five-forbidden-moves}).\n\\begin{enumerate}\n\
    \\item\\emph{(Faithfulness.)}\n$F$ restricted to $\\operatorname{Cat}_{\\T}(\\Elayer{2})\n \\setminus \\bigcup_{i} \\\
    operatorname{ker}(M_{i})$ is faithful.\n\n\\item\\emph{(Degeneration.)}\nFor each $i$, there exist $X_{i} \\neq Y_{i}$\
    \ with\n$F(X_{i}) \\cong F(Y_{i})$ in $\\operatorname{Mod}(\\mathrm{ZFC})$.\n\n\\item\\emph{(Completeness.)}\nEvery identification\
