    # Hardy-Littlewood Constant


    ## Summary


    Hardy-Littlewood twin constant C₂(k) = ∏ p(p-2)/(p-1)² for p≥3. C₂(2)=3/4, C₂(3)=45/64. Decreasing toward C₂≈1.3203.


    ## Statement


    ```latex

    \label{def:hl-constant}

    The twin prime constant truncated at depth~$k$:

    \[

    C_2(k) = \prod_{\substack{i=2 \\ p_i \ge 3}}^{k}

    \frac{p_i(p_i - 2)}{(p_i - 1)^2}.

    \]

    Computed as a rational (numerator, denominator).

    $C_2(2) = 3/4$, $C_2(3) = 45/64$.


    \textbf{Lean:} \texttt{hl\_twin\_constant\_approx}.\quad

    \textbf{Registry:} III.D106.

    ```
