    # BSD Functional


    ## Summary


    BSD_τ(k) = rank(k) · L’_τ(1,k); measures proto-code density at each primorial level


    ## Statement


    ```latex

    \label{def:bsd-functional}

    The \emph{BSD functional} is the tower-indexed quantity

    \begin{equation}

    \operatorname{BSD}_{\T}(k) \;=\;

    r(k) \cdot L_{\T}''(1, k),

    \label{eq:ch46-bsd-functional}

    \end{equation}

    where $r(k) = \operatorname{rk}_{\mathbb{Z}}(G_k / G_k^{\mathrm{tor}})$ is

    the rank function (Definition~\ref{def:rank-as-tower-depth}, Ch.~45) and

    $L_{\T}''(1, k) = \frac{d}{ds} L_{\T}(s, k)\big|_{s=1}$ is the derivative of

    the spectral determinant at the symmetry point~$s = 1$.  The functional is

    defined for each finite primorial depth $k \geq 1$.

    ```
