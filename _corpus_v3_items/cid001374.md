    \          $\\dim Q < \\dim T^2 = 2$.\n    \\item[\\textup{(M3)}] \\textbf{Crossing point.}\n          The quotient admits\

    \\emph{Step~1.}\nConstraint~(M2) forces $\\dim Q \\leq 1$,\nso the quotient must collapse at least one direction.\n\n\\\
