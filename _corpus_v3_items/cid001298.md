    \        \\begin{itemize}\n              \\item The base components $(D, A)$\n                    of $f(x)$\n        \

    \          In short: \\emph{base maps to base,\n          fiber maps to fiber}.\n\n    \\item[\\textup{(TA3)}]\n     \

    \    with \\textbf{finitely many} active spectral\n          coefficients\n          (II.T31, Chapter~\\ref{ch:canonical-basis}).\n\
