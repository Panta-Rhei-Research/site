    ## Statement


    ```latex

    \label{def:eratosthenes-sieve}

    $\mathrm{sieve}(n) : \{0, \ldots, n\} \to \{\mathrm{true}, \mathrm{false}\}$

    such that $\mathrm{sieve}(k) = \mathrm{true}$ if and only if $k$ is prime.
    ```
