    The latter objects have finite depth.\nTherefore $\\omega$ is the unique object of infinite depth ---\nthe unique infinity.\n\
    ```\n\n## Proof / Justification\n\n```latex\nBy the Ontic Closure Theorem\n(Theorem~\\ref{thm:ontic-closure}, I.T01),\n\
    \\[\n    \\Obj(\\tau)\n    = \\{\\omega\\}\n    \\cup O_\\alpha \\cup O_\\pi \\cup O_\\gamma \\cup O_\\eta,\n\\]\nand\
