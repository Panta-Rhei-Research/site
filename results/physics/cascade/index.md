The physics cascade route is preserved for compatibility. The canonical public
surface is now the [Calibration Cascade]({{ '/results/calibration-cascade/' | relative_url }}).

The new page separates three roles that were previously too easy to blur:

- the **Numerical Prediction Catalogue** lists public claims;
- the **constants ledger** is a table of source outputs;
- the **Calibration Cascade** records dependency paths, source mappings, unit
  contexts, comparison vintage, Registry links, TauLib modules, and verification
  surfaces.

This bridge deliberately avoids treating the legacy physics cascade as a
standalone ontology. For the current review-facing dependency overlay, use:
