> Current τ responses to the Physics Structural Challenge Ledger.

Physics preserves the **102-item external unsolved-physics source feed** because physics is the most mature, formalized, measurement-facing public stress surface. The feed is refactored into ring depths **R0–R6** and subdomain clusters; ring depth, not raw count, determines structural priority.

A separate **15-item τ-native physics audit set** tests formal-to-empirical readout, metrology and SI anchoring, hidden-fit control, gauge ontology, symmetry breaking and sector selection, renormalization and scale decoupling, measurement and the classical limit, locality and no-signaling, vacuum/ground-state ontology, time asymmetry and low-entropy boundary, information/entropy/capacity bounds, emergence and collective laws, formalization equivalence, and physical preconditions for life.

## Status discipline

An internally addressed τ response is **not** an externally settled physics result. Physics-facing challenges remain bridge claims and empirical-accountability claims until measurement, prediction timing, and external review settle them.

## What this surface reports

For each Physics structural challenge, the response page reports:

- **Response status** — internally addressed, partially addressed, structurally constrained, further investigation, backlog, or external recovery checkpoint
- **Ring depth (R0–R6)** — structural priority within the imported physics feed
- **Subdomain cluster** — root foundations, particle/SM, cosmology, QCD/nuclear, fluid/plasma, condensed matter, quantum gravity, quantum information, astrophysics, peripheral phenomena, physics–life–mind boundary
- **Imported vs τ-native badge** — whether this is an imported external feed item or a τ-native audit challenge
- **Prediction / falsification anchors** — links to numerical predictions and named falsification tests
- **Evidence route** — Construction Spine step, Registry anchors, TauLib modules
- **Verification route** — bridge verification, empirical accountability, prediction timing
- **External-review boundary** — what would need external measurement or peer review to settle
