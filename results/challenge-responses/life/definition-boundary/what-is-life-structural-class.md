# What Is Life as a Structural Class? — Challenge Response

**Structural Challenge:** [What Is Life as a Structural Class?](/agenda/structural-challenge-ledger/life/definition-boundary/what-is-life-structural-class/) (`LIFE-SC-01`)
**Domain:** Life · **Cluster:** definition boundary
**Response status:** **Internally addressed**

## Challenge

Can life be defined as a structural class rather than as an empirical checklist of terrestrial organisms? A viable theory of life must distinguish living systems from nonliving systems, liminal cases, artificial systems, ecosystems, and nonterran candidates without relying only on Earth-biological examples.

## τ response

Show that Life = Distinction + SelfDesc is not merely a slogan. The framework must demonstrate that the two predicates recover the classical hallmarks of life, generate useful phase boundaries, and make biological structure legible without reducing life to an arbitrary list. Mapping to current τ work pending fresh registry curation.
