> Current τ responses to the Life Structural Challenge Ledger.

The Life ledger is **not** a raw import of all biological and neuroscience puzzles. It contains **29 structural challenges** testing life as such across five clusters:

1. **Definition, boundary, persistence** — what makes a thing alive; the life/non-life phase boundary; metabolism, homeostasis, repair; boundary and individuation.
2. **Origin, substrate, life-as-could-be** — abiogenesis; substrate independence; structural favorability vs contingency; extraterrestrial biosignatures; artificial and synthetic life.
3. **Molecular code architecture** — central dogma; folding and sequence-to-structure; the genetic code's origin and structure; biological homochirality; protocell membranes; universal energy currency.
4. **Evolution, morphology, tree** — LUCA; tree-of-life topology; major evolutionary transitions; sexual reproduction; biological time and rhythms; morphogenesis and body-plan; aging; open-ended evolution.
5. **Ecology, cognition, life–mind bridge** — ecosystems and superorganisms; agency and decision-making; neural computation; cosmic life carriers; consciousness and the life–mind bridge.

## Status discipline

An internally addressed τ response is **not** an externally settled biological result. Life-facing challenges remain explanatory-adequacy claims, bridge claims, and empirical-correlate claims until biological replication, measurement, and external review settle them.

## What this surface reports

For each Life structural challenge, the response page reports:

- **Response status** — internally addressed, partially addressed, structurally constrained, further investigation, backlog, out of scope, or external recovery checkpoint
- **Cluster** — one of the five clusters above
- **K_χ branch anchor** — chirality, energy, information, temporal, or phenomenal branch where applicable (Book VI, Kinetic Pseudoscalar Channel LG-Y02 / VI.L18)
- **Evidence route** — Construction Spine step (CS-07), Registry anchors, biological correlates
- **Verification route** — Life Core Semantics alignment, structural biology mapping, biomarker correlates
- **External-review boundary** — what would need biological replication or peer review to settle

## Token-level v1 archival

Token-specific v1 raw-feed items (Blue whale, Forest rings, G-spot, Adult form of Facetotecta, Adult form of Planctosphaera pelagica, Function of vanadocytes, Gall-inducing insects, Butterfly migration, etc.) are **not canonical structural challenges**. They are preserved in raw-source archives only where useful for provenance.

The canonical 29-challenge set was curated for **structural relevance**: each challenge tests something the framework's life layer must be able to express, classify, address, defer, or refuse with reason.
