> Current τ responses to the Metaphysics Structural Challenge Ledger.

The Metaphysics ledger contains **29 E₃ challenges** organized into four clusters:

1. **Ontology, modality, reality** — being and "something rather than nothing"; modality and counterfactuals; identity and persistence; universals; causation and lawhood; time and temporality; mathematical objects and abstract reality; structural realism; six ontic requirements; objecthood and individuation.
2. **Knowledge, truth, logic, language** — truth and truthmakers; knowledge (Gettier); justification regress; vagueness and sorites; meaning and intentionality; demarcation; induction and underdetermination; implication and material consequence.
3. **Mind, consciousness, selfhood, agency** — the hard problem of consciousness; qualia; mind-body relation; personal identity; perception and Molyneux's problem; free will and responsibility; cognition, AI, and artificial mind.
4. **Normativity, religion, commitment** — moral knowledge and normative truth; dignity and the categorical imperative; God, logos, ultimate reality; the proof–commitment boundary (No Forced Stance); moral luck.

## Status discipline

A metaphysical Challenge Response is **not** laboratory proof and **not** Lean proof. It checks whether the program's stance is explicit, coherent with the construction, disciplined by the no-externalities rule, and honest about diagrammatic access.

The **No Forced Stance theorem (VII.T47)** is the framework's structural floor: τ cannot force a stance on the ω-germ question, and any such stance belongs to the commitment register (Reg_C), not to proof.

## What this surface reports

For each Metaphysics structural challenge, the response page reports:

- **Response status** — internally addressed, partially addressed, structurally constrained, further investigation, backlog, out of scope, or external recovery checkpoint
- **Cluster** — one of the four clusters above
- **Book VII register tags** — `Reg_E` (empirical), `Reg_P` (proof), `Reg_D` (diagrammatic), `Reg_C` (commitment)
- **Ontic requirements** — `OR1`–`OR6` narrowing principles
- **Evidence route** — Construction Spine step (CS-08, CS-09, CS-10), Registry anchors, categorical architecture
- **Verification route** — diagrammatic adequacy, conceptual coherence, commitment-boundary disclosure
- **External-review boundary** — what would need philosophical scrutiny or commitment-level engagement to settle

## What this surface does not claim

A metaphysical Challenge Response **must not** imply direct noumenal access, final ontology, or external acceptance unless those stronger statuses are separately earned. Stances belonging to the commitment register (Reg_C) are explicitly marked as such and not collapsed into proof.
