## Status discipline

A Challenge Response reports the program's current internal stance against a canonical Structural Challenge. It does **not** imply external verification, scientific consensus, philosophical settlement, or final proof. Read each response together with its evidence route, registry references, verification status, and external-review boundary.

<aside class="callout muted" style="font-size: 0.92rem; border-left: 3px solid var(--lane-current); padding: 0.6rem 0.9rem; margin: 1rem 0;"><strong>Reading the metaphysics status badges honestly.</strong> 24 of 30 metaphysics CRs carry the <strong>Structurally constrained</strong> badge — the highest concentration in the four-domain matrix. That label is deliberately weaker than "Internally addressed": it means the τ framework offers a structural account, reframing, or material constraint on the problem, not that the problem is settled. The status ladder reads (strongest → weakest): <strong>Internally addressed</strong> (τ closes the question on its own terms) → <strong>Structurally constrained</strong> (τ reframes or partially answers; the canonical philosophical formulation remains open) → <strong>Partially addressed</strong> (τ captures one face of the question, others open) → <strong>Further investigation</strong> (τ-internal route exists but is incomplete) → <strong>External recovery checkpoint</strong> (handed back to non-τ method by design). For consciousness, normativity, and the hard ontological questions, "Structurally constrained" is the honest top of the ladder — these are exactly the questions where philosophical traditions disagree on what counts as "settling," and the program does not claim to have ended that disagreement. Open the per-CR page to read which response sub-status applies and what the <em>external-review boundary</em> says — that's where the framework states what an external philosopher would still find genuinely open. See also question 13 in the <a href="{{ '/program/about/red-team-faq/' | relative_url }}">Red-team FAQ</a> on whether Book VII <em>solves</em> the hard problem of consciousness or <em>relocates</em> it (the program's own answer: relocates).</aside>
