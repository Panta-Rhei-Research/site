## What this surface reports

For each canonical Structural Challenge in the SCL, the corresponding Challenge Response page reports:

- **Response status** — internally addressed, partially addressed, structurally constrained, further investigation, backlog, out of scope, or external recovery checkpoint
- **Evidence route** — canonical Registry items the response is supported by (with confidence)
- **Verification** — verification route (formal proof checking, bridge verification, empirical accountability, categorical register check, commitment register audit) + status
- **External-review boundary** — what would need external work to settle
- **Provenance** — link to v1 problem-ledger predecessor where one exists

## Source-of-truth discipline

The canonical source for each Challenge Response lives in the corpus repo under `structural-challenge-ledger/responses/{domain}/{cluster}/{slug}.md`. The pages on this site are projections produced by the Atlas R5 export pipeline and should not be edited directly here.
