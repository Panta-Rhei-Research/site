> Results-side projection of the canonical Structural Challenge Ledger.

Challenge Responses are the Results-side projection of the [Structural Challenge Ledger]({{ '/agenda/structural-challenge-ledger/' | relative_url }}).

The Agenda-side Structural Challenge Ledger states what the framework must face. This Results-side surface records the current τ response: whether the challenge is internally addressed, partially addressed, structurally constrained, under further investigation, in backlog, out of scope, or an external recovery checkpoint.

A Challenge Response does not mean external verification, scientific acceptance, philosophical settlement, or final proof. It reports the program's current internal stance, evidence route, verification route, bridge burden, and external-review boundary.

## Status grammar

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Internally addressed | The program has an internal answer; external verification remains a separate question |
| Partially addressed | Some structural progress; full response not yet earned |
| Structurally constrained | The challenge is constrained by τ structure but not fully resolved within it |
| Further investigation | Active research; no current public stance |
| Backlog | Recognized challenge; not yet addressed |
| Out of scope | Falls outside the program's declared scope, with reason |
| External recovery checkpoint | Status depends on external (non-τ) work |

These status labels are separate from verification state and from external acceptance.

## What this surface does not claim

- It does not claim τ has solved every challenge.
- It does not imply external consensus, peer-review settlement, or scientific acceptance.
- It does not promote imported open problems to solved status.
- It does not collapse structural constraint into proof.
