## Construction role

Book VII begins where the consumer/self-modeling capacity is internalized as an
object of study. The transition is not merely carrying and decoding code, but
modeling that code-carrying and decoding and reasoning about the conditions
under which self-modeling is possible. Reflection is therefore a new layer
function, not a poetic description of intelligence.
