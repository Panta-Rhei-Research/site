## Construction role

Life is defined by two earned predicates. Distinction is the sharp, stable,
internally maintained boundary between self and non-self. SelfDesc is the
internal code-reading evaluator that reconstructs it. Classical hallmarks such
as metabolism, growth, response, reproduction, and evolution become consequences
or calibrations of the predicate pair, not axioms smuggled in from biology.
