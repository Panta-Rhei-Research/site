## Construction role

Logic and inference become internal reflective tools rather than external
sovereigns. Boolean, Bayesian, modal, paraconsistent, and proof-theoretic
readouts occupy typed roles under the register discipline. Truth is therefore
not flattened into one metalogical predicate; it is handled through the
register, witness, and boundary conditions under which a claim can be
inspected.
