## Smale-derived structural challenges

> Smale's list is not imported wholesale. We use it as a curated source for structural mathematical challenges: problems that test arithmetic addressability, algebraic complexity, dynamical stability, global invertibility, optimisation, spectral distribution, and the formal limits of intelligence. Items already covered by Clay are not duplicated. Solved items are treated as recovery checkpoints.
